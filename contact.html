Before You Write

How quickly will you reply?

We aim to respond to all messages within 24 to 48 hours on business days. Partnership and business enquiries may take slightly longer.

I want to submit a tool — where do I go?

The quickest route is our dedicated Submit a Tool page. Alternatively, use the form here and select "Tool Submission" as the topic.

Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content?

We do not currently publish guest posts. Sponsored listings are not available as this would compromise the independence of the directory.

My tool is listed incorrectly — what should I do?

Use the form above, select "Report an incorrect listing", and describe the issue. We will review and update it promptly.

Can I advertise on AI Tools Directory?

We serve ads through Google AdSense. For direct advertising enquiries, please use the contact form and select "Business or Partnership Enquiry".

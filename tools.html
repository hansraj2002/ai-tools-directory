About These Free Tools

This page offers a set of practical browser-based utilities that work without any account, download, or data upload. Everything runs locally in your browser — your text is never sent to a server or stored anywhere.

Word & Character Counter

Writers, students, and content creators frequently need to know the word count of their text, especially when working within limits for essays, social media posts, or SEO content. Our counter also tracks sentence count and provides an estimated reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

Password Generator

Weak passwords are one of the leading causes of account breaches. Our password generator creates cryptographically random passwords using the character sets you choose. Generated passwords are never transmitted or stored — they exist only in your browser for the duration of your session.

Keyword Ideas Generator

Before writing content or running an SEO campaign, knowing which keyword variations to target helps you reach the right audience. This tool generates a list of related long-tail and short-tail keyword ideas based on your input topic, giving you a starting point for research.

Readability Checker

Clear writing communicates more effectively. Our readability checker analyses your text for average sentence length, word complexity indicators, and estimated reading grade level. Shorter sentences and simpler vocabulary typically lead to better engagement and comprehension.

Text Summariser

When you need to quickly extract the key points from a long piece of text, our summariser identifies and extracts the most informative sentences automatically. It is useful for research, note-taking, and quickly digesting long articles.

LinkedIn Message Helper

Cold outreach on LinkedIn has a low response rate when messages are generic. Personalised, concise messages that clearly state your intent perform significantly better. This tool generates a tailored connection message based on the recipient's name, role, and your specific goal.
